Frequently Asked Questions

Are all online tools working right now?

Passport Photo Maker is available in this release. Other photo, PDF and document tools will be connected in later development stages.

Can I use this website on mobile and computer?

Yes. The homepage and Passport Photo Maker are responsive and adapt to mobile phones, tablets and desktop screens.

Can customers create an account?

Customer registration and login are planned for a later stage. They are not connected in this frontend-only version.

Can I place a print order online?

Online order submission, payment and order tracking will be added after the customer and admin modules are built.
