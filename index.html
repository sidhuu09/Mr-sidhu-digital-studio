Frequently Asked Questions

Which tools are available?

Passport Photo Maker, Photo Crop & Resize, Background Remover and A4 + 4×6 Photo Print are included in this version.

Can I use this website on mobile?

Yes. The website is responsive. Printing options depend on your device, browser and connected printer.

Does the website print automatically without confirmation?

No. The website prepares the print layout and opens the browser print dialog. You must confirm the print job.
